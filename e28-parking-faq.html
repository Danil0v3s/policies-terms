Sky e28 Parking Help

Answers to common questions about the Sky e28 Parking app, which books and cancels parking spots on your existing MobbiPro account. The app is independent and is not made by MobbiPro.

Getting started

How do I sign in?

Enter your MobbiPro email and password. The server address is already filled in. The app signs in, checks that your account is allowed to make reservations, and then saves your login on your device.

Is my password safe?

Your login is stored on your device only, and the app talks directly to MobbiPro. It does not send your details to any other server. Signing out in Settings removes them from the app.

The app says my session expired.

MobbiPro logs you out from time to time. The app signs in again by itself using your saved login. If that fails, for example because you changed your password, sign out in Settings and sign in again.

Booking

How do I book a spot?

Open the Book tab, choose the seat type (Car, Moto or Bike), tick the days you want, and tap Book. Each day then shows whether it was booked or why it was not.

Can I book several days at once?

Yes. The app books each selected day one after the other, with a short pause between them. "Furthest day" selects the last day MobbiPro currently allows. "Check" runs all the checks without booking anything.

What time does a booking start and end?

Future days are booked from 00:00 to 23:59, Lisbon time. Today is booked from the moment you book until 23:59.

What do "Booked", "Full" and the free spot numbers mean?

"Booked" means you already have a booking that day. Every other day shows how many spots are free for the selected seat type, or "Full" if none are left.

Why was a day refused?

The message shows MobbiPro's reason. Common ones are that the day is full, that you already have a booking that day, that the day is outside the booking window, or that none of your vehicles fits the selected seat type.

What does "Pending approval" mean?

Some spot types need approval from MobbiPro. The booking has been requested but is not confirmed until it is approved.

Bookings

Why does one booking cover several days?

MobbiPro merges bookings on back-to-back days into one. If you book Wednesday and Friday and then Thursday, you get a single booking from Wednesday to Friday. The Bookings tab shows both ends, for example "Wed 18 Nov – Fri 20 Nov" and "Wed 14:33 – Fri 23:59", with the number of days. Every day it covers shows as booked on the Book tab.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open the Bookings tab and tap Cancel on the booking. To cancel several at once, tap Select, choose the bookings, and confirm once.

What happens if I cancel a booking that has already started?

The spot is freed from that moment on. The time before stays in your MobbiPro history.

What happens if I cancel a multi-day booking?

Cancelling from the Bookings tab frees every remaining day of that booking. Cancelling only today, from Siri or the Apple Watch, frees today until midnight and keeps the following days booked.

Why can't I cancel a booking?

It shows "Locked". MobbiPro does not allow that booking to be cancelled.

Vehicles and settings

I have more than one vehicle. Which one is used?

A spot only accepts its own vehicle type: Car spots take cars, Moto spots take motorcycles and Bike spots take bicycles. In Settings, under Vehicles, tap a vehicle to make it your default, or choose Automatic to use MobbiPro's main vehicle. The default is used when it fits the seat type.
